A transparent planning model

Each use draws the hot-water share needed to reach your tap temperature. Recovery adds heat between and during events. The model treats the tank as evenly mixed, so real stratified tanks, heat-pump conditions and pipe losses can behave differently.

Tap-water capacity: tank litres × (stored °C − cold °C) ÷ (tap °C − cold °C)

Recovery: heater heat output is converted to equivalent tank litres using water's heat capacity over the selected temperature rise.

All estimates use your inputs. They do not select a product, verify a model rating or replace a plumber's sizing assessment.

Why use a schedule?

Daily litres don’t show a morning rush.

Two homes can use the same amount of hot water in a day and need different storage capacity. Long or back-to-back showers can draw down a tank before its heater has time to recover. This planner lets you change the timing, flow and heater output to see how those assumptions affect the result.

Start with your fixture flow rate and the heater’s published water-heating output. If you do not know the output, use zero for a storage-only comparison.

A few useful details

Questions people ask

What does “tap-water capacity” mean?

It estimates how many litres at your selected tap temperature could be mixed from a full tank at its stored temperature and the cold-water temperature you entered. It is a heat-balance estimate, not the exact first-hour delivery printed for a heater.

What if I don’t know my tank’s heating output?

Set output to 0 kW. The planner will show demand against stored heat without giving credit for recovery. If a product label lists recovery in litres per hour, convert it to thermal output only if you know the test conditions; otherwise ask the supplier or installer.

Can I use this to choose or install a water heater?

No. It is a scenario planner for comparing assumptions. The estimate does not account for tank stratification, first-hour rating, heat-pump derating, pipe losses, plumbing layout, regulations or all usage patterns. Have a licensed plumber size and install a system.

Does this upload my household schedule?

No. The planner runs in your browser and saves settings only in this browser’s local storage. A share link encodes the scenario in the URL, so share it only with people you choose.
